ok so I was back at school but it was also the airport, like the big hall at Perth airport but with the year 9 lockers all along the walls. everyone had already gone and I was meant to be catching a flight but I didn't know where to. the carpet was that green school carpet. I went down a corridor to find the gate and it just kept going, every door had the same number, 14, and when I went through one I was back at the start of the same corridor. there was a hum the whole time like the lights. mum was there at one point I think, or someone who was mum, at the end of the corridor, but I never got any closer. it was late afternoon but also dark outside the windows somehow. then the corridor was the indoor pool from swimming lessons and the water was completely still and I knew I wasn't allowed in. not scared exactly, more like I'd forgotten something really important. when I turned around the lockers were gone. can't remember the end, I think I just stood there
