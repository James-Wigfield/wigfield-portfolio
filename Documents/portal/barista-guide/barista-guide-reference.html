Six months in. Time for a proper clean.A care guide for your Breville Barista Express Impress

Below: what each tool in the box is for, the everyday routine that keeps it running, and a one-hour deep clean you can tick off as you go.

  • Maintenance light flashing: run a cleaning cycle (tablet)
  • Maintenance light solid: time to descale (powder)
Start the deep clean

What came in the box

Four things in your photo, each with one job.

Two more parts you’ll need, probably in the storage tray behind the drip tray: the round silicone cleaning disc (needed for every cleaning cycle) and a small Allen key (only for changing the shower screen seal).

The routine

Most of this takes seconds. The milk wand habit matters most, because dried milk is what you’re seeing now.

  1. Every milk drink
    • Wipe the wand with a damp cloth straight away.
    • Point it at the drip tray and turn the dial to steam for 2 seconds to blow milk out of the inside.
  2. Every coffee
    • Knock out the puck, then rinse the basket and portafilter under hot water.
    • Before your shot, press 1 CUP for a short flush through the group head.
  3. Daily
    • Top up the tank with fresh cold tap water. Don’t use distilled or heavily filtered water.
    • Empty the drip tray when the “Empty me!” float pops up.
    • Before the first coffee, hold DOSE for a second to purge stale grounds, then tip them out.
  4. Weekly
    • Wash the drip tray and grill in warm soapy water.
    • Wipe the shower screen (the metal disc above the portafilter) with a damp cloth.
    • Brush loose grounds from the tamp area.
  5. Light flashes
    • Run a cleaning cycle with a tablet (step 6). For a coffee a day this tends to land around monthly.
    • Brush out the grinder burrs at the same time (step 3).
  6. Every 3 months
    • Replace the water filter in the tank (step 5). Sooner if your water is hard.
  7. Light stays solid
    • Descale (step 4). How often depends on your water; a fresh filter stretches the gap.

Fixing the dried milk

A little crust on the wand is normal and comes off easily. This is the short version of step 2.

  1. Turn the machine off and let the wand cool.
  2. Wrap a cloth soaked in very hot water around the wand for 2–3 minutes. The milk softens and wipes off.
  3. Push the pin of the steam wand tool into each hole in the tip.
  4. Still crusty? Unscrew the tip with the tool’s spanner, soak it in hot water for 10 minutes, pin the holes again and screw it back on.

Never use a scourer, steel wool or abrasive cream on the wand. If it builds up again often, an espresso-machine milk cleaner is a good soak for the tip.

The six-month deep clean

About an hour, much of it waiting. Do the steps in this order: the descale needs the water filter out, and the new filter goes in before the cleaning cycle. Tap a step to open it.

0 of 8 done
1

Set upDrip tray, filter out, find the disc

2 min
  1. Turn the machine off. If you’ve just used it, give it 20 minutes to cool.
  2. Empty and rinse the drip tray, then slide it back in.
  3. Lift out the water tank, pull out the filter holder and remove the old filter. Empty the tank.
  4. Find the round silicone cleaning disc. It’s usually in the storage tray behind the drip tray.

The filter must be out before you descale, or it soaks up the descaler.

2

Rescue the milk wandSoften, pin, soak the tip

15 min
  1. Check the steam dial is on standby and the machine is off and cool.
  2. Wrap a cloth soaked in very hot water around the wand for 2–3 minutes, then wipe the milk off.
  3. Push the pin of the steam wand tool into each hole in the tip.
  4. Fit the tool’s spanner over the tip and unscrew it. Drop it in a cup of hot water.
  5. Move on to step 3 while it soaks, then come back: pin the holes, rinse, and screw the tip back on firmly.
3

Brush the grinder and tampBurrs, chute, tamp face

10 min
  1. Unlock the bean hopper, hold it over a container and turn its dial to let the beans out. Lock it back on.
  2. Put the portafilter in the cradle and hold DOSE until the grinder runs empty. Tip out the grounds.
  3. Remove the hopper. Unlock the top burr and lift it out by its wire handle.
  4. Brush the top burr and the lower burr with the cleaning brush. No water.
  5. Pull off the tamp cover and brush out the chute.
  6. Push the tamp lever down and hold it. Brush around the tamp face, then let the lever up slowly with the brush clear.
  7. Refit the tamp cover, top burr and hopper, and add your beans back.

The burrs are very sharp. Don’t turn the number ring on the top burr: that changes your grind range.

4

DescaleSachet, then a fresh-water flush

20 min
  1. Stir the descaler sachet into 1 litre of water until fully dissolved. Pour it into the tank and refit it.
  2. With the machine off, hold 2 CUP + POWER for 5 seconds. The buttons flash while it heats.
  3. It’s ready when the power and 2 cup buttons and both indicator lights are solid.
  4. Put a jug under the group head and press 2 CUP. It runs for about 25 seconds.
  5. Hold the jug under the steam wand and turn the dial right to steam (about 13 seconds).
  6. Hold the jug under the hot water spout and turn the dial left to hot water (about 8 seconds).
  7. Repeat steps 4–6 until the tank is empty. Empty the jug and drip tray as you go.
  8. Rinse the tank well, fill it to max (2 litres) with fresh cold water, and run the same rounds again to flush out the solution.
  9. Press POWER to exit descale mode.

If you leave it idle for 5 minutes it switches off. Restart from the 5-second button hold.

5

Fit a new water filterOverdue by now

6 min
  1. Soak the new filter in a cup of cold water for 5 minutes.
  2. Rinse it under cold running water for 5 seconds.
  3. Clip it into the filter holder and set the date dial 3 months ahead.
  4. Push the holder down onto the post in the tank until it locks. Fill the tank and refit it.

No spare filter yet? Skip this step, fill the tank and carry on. Pick up a Breville replacement filter soon.

6

Run a cleaning cycleDisc and one tablet

7 min
  1. Make sure the tank is full and the drip tray is empty.
  2. Put the 1 cup basket in the portafilter, the silicone disc on top, and one tablet in the middle of the disc.
  3. Lock the portafilter in and put a large container underneath.
  4. Turn the machine off, then hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP + POWER for 3 seconds.
  5. It runs for about 5 minutes, pulsing water in and flushing it back. Little or no water should come out of the spouts.
  6. Remove the portafilter. If the tablet hasn’t fully dissolved, run the cycle again.
  7. Take the disc out, lock the portafilter back in and press 2 CUP to rinse. Wipe off any splashes.
7

Soak the baskets and portafilterYour second tablet

20 min
  1. Dissolve the second tablet in a bowl of hot water.
  2. Soak all four filter baskets and the metal head of the portafilter for about 20 minutes. Keep the handle out of the water if you can.
  3. Rinse everything thoroughly. If any basket holes are still blocked, clear them with the pin.
8

Wipe down and finishShower screen, trays, outside

10 min
  1. Wipe the shower screen and the inside of the group head with a damp cloth.
  2. Fit an empty basket, lock in the portafilter and hold 1 CUP for a short rinse.
  3. Wash the drip tray, grill, grounds separator and “Empty me!” float in warm soapy water. Wipe out the storage tray.
  4. Wipe the outside with a soft damp cloth and buff it dry.
  5. Pull one shot and pour it away before making a coffee to drink.

Avoid these, and restock

Don’t

  • Put any part in the dishwasher. Hand-wash everything.
  • Use scourers, steel wool or abrasive creams. They scratch the steel and the matte finish.
  • Use harsh or alkaline cleaners on the body. Mild dish soap and a soft cloth only.
  • Descale with vinegar, or with the water filter still in the tank.
  • Fill the tank with hot, distilled or demineralised water.
  • Wash the grinder burrs with water. Brush only.

Buy before you run out

  • Cleaning tabletsThe box only had two. You’ll want a tub.
  • Descaler sachetsOne per descale. The box only had one.
  • Water filtersOne every 3 months.
  • Shower screen sealReplace when water leaks around the portafilter or it locks in loosely. The Allen key is for this.
