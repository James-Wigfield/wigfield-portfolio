Team relay warm-up

Two lines, drink and flip your cup rim-up before the next person goes. Loud, fast, forgiving — the perfect opener for the early crowd.

Beer Pong

All-day knockout bracket

A running tournament up on the balcony. Sink the cups, bounce shots count double, winners stay on. Group stage early, finals after dark.

Format 2v2Bounce = 2 cups
Living Room

Stack Cup ★

Bounce-and-pass survival

Everyone circles a table, bounce a ball into your cup then pass clockwise. Catch the person ahead and they cop a stacked cup. Last stack standing drinks it.

Players 8–14Signature chaos

Ring of Fire card circle

Spread the deck around the king's cup. Each card is a rule — waterfall, mate, never-have-I. The fourth king pours, the fourth king downs it. A breather between sprints.

House Rules

01One ref per game. Disputes end fast — the ref's call is final, no replays.
02Water between rounds. A cup of water for every game you play, no exceptions. The jug lives at the beer station.
03Points carry, people drift. Leave early or arrive late — your tally stays on the board.
04Plastic by the lake only. No glass anywhere near the water or the garden race.
05Last orders at the toast. Cups down for the birthday toast at 8:15, then awards.

Keep It a Good Day

  • Pace beats points. The Golden Cup isn't worth feeling rough — drink water, eat at 5, sit a round out whenever.
  • The lake is for looking at. No swimming, and nobody near the edge once they've been drinking. The race stays on the grass.
  • Mind the early leavers & drivers. Sort lifts or stays before the first cup. Keep soft drinks stocked at the depot.
  • Look out for each other. If someone's done, they're done — get them water, a seat, and some food. No pressure, ever.
